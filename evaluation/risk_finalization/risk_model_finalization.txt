9. LIMITATIONS FOR REPORTING
----------------------------
- The fixed holdout contains only 243 rows; a single split does not establish broad statistical significance.
- Labels are project-level annotations, not clinically validated ground truth.
- This internal Human Review is not expert or clinical validation and cannot establish that original test labels are wrong.
- Persistent boundary cases remain among risk categories, especially risk_passive versus risk_suicidal_ideation.
- Augmentation data was generated and screened within the project; its coverage and provenance limit generalization.
- Better aggregate metrics do not demonstrate clinical safety or safe behavior for every risk category.
- V2's ideation recall equals v1's and remains below baseline, despite higher overall scores.
